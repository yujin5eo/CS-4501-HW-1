# Intentional anti-UX violations

The lecture PDFs were not available in this repository. Page citations below reproduce the supplied PDF-page references and were **not independently checked**. These are 20 implementation candidates, not a claim of 20 wholly independent effects: items 3/4/6 overlap in visual organization; 5/19 overlap in search; 11/12 overlap in memory; and 13/14 overlap in reading. Even merging these conservatively leaves well over ten distinct mechanisms.

Screenshot filenames below are the required capture plan. Capture remains pending because this environment had no browser and blocked browser installation; no evidence was fabricated.

1. **Color discrimination** — *Perception 1, pp. 14–21.* **Location:** Seasonal Allocations swatches and selected delivery card. **Behavior:** small blue/green/violet states differ subtly while text labels remain legible. **Mechanism/impact:** close hues and small area slow perceptual discrimination. **Recovery:** read stable `Allocated`/`Selected` text. **Evidence:** `catalog.png`.
2. **Feedback away from focus** — *Perception 1, pp. 34–38.* **Location:** Ledger versus sticky Bee Notices above navigation. **Behavior:** genuine validation errors appear only in that persistent remote strip and input is retained. **Mechanism/impact:** visual attention at the form may miss remote feedback. **Recovery:** read Bee Notices, correct, resubmit. **Evidence:** `invalid-notice.png`.
3. **Misleading proximity/common region** — *Perception 2, pp. 41–49.* **Location:** catalog. **Behavior:** an unrelated Auntie Bee announcement shares the quantity region while wrap is separated far below. **Mechanism/impact:** grouping suggests false relationships and separates real ones. **Recovery:** explicit labels remain associated. **Evidence:** `catalog.png`.
4. **Misleading similarity** — *Perception 2, pp. 52–53.* **Location:** right rail and catalog cards. **Behavior:** announcements and actionable allocation cards share bordered, muted treatments. Decorative cards make no action claim. **Mechanism/impact:** role requires reading rather than visual category recognition. **Recovery:** actionable controls are actual labelled buttons. **Evidence:** `anti-home.png`.
5. **Weak information scent/conflicting expectations** — *Perception 2, pp. 18–35; Attention 1, pp. 36–41.* **Location:** primary Flowers link and facts page. **Behavior:** Flowers leads to facts; purchasing is called Seasonal Allocations. Facts explicitly point to the rail. **Mechanism/impact:** conventional catalog expectation conflicts with destination and weak label scent slows navigation. **Recovery:** explanatory facts paragraph. **Evidence:** `facts.png`.
6. **Banner blindness** — *Attention 2, pp. 15–16.* **Location:** right rail. **Behavior:** the real Seasonal Allocations route sits in a promotional-looking gradient panel between farm notices. **Mechanism/impact:** learned ad filtering may suppress attention despite high visibility. **Recovery:** panel is stable, labelled, and keyboard reachable. **Evidence:** `anti-home.png`.
7. **Peripheral distraction** — *Perception 1, pp. 29–33; Attention 1, pp. 5–13.* **Location:** viewport edges. **Behavior:** two irrelevant bees drift gently without covering controls. **Mechanism/impact:** peripheral motion can capture attention. **Recovery:** motion is irrelevant, nonblocking, and disabled by `prefers-reduced-motion`. **Evidence:** `catalog.png` (static frame).
8. **Inconsistent familiar structure** — *Attention 2, pp. 7–14; Memory 1, pp. 46–55.* **Location:** catalog, delivery, checkout. **Behavior:** fixed nav order reverses on Flight and spacing changes on Ledger; header/cart relation differs from content. **Mechanism/impact:** learned spatial positions fail across views. **Recovery:** destinations and labels remain functional and never react to pointer movement. **Evidence:** `delivery.png`, `checkout.png`.
9. **Change blindness** — *Attention 1, pp. 15–19.* **Location:** product selection/header. **Behavior:** clicking Mark allocation silently changes only the already-visible distant `Ledger marks` value. **Mechanism/impact:** attention stays near the action and may miss the unanimated remote state change. **Recovery:** persistent header count and session state. **Evidence sequence:** `cart-before.png` → click Mark allocation → `cart-after.png`.
10. **Poor chunking of a long number** — *Perception 2, p. 77; Memory 1, pp. 7–11.* **Location:** Wedding Circular. **Behavior:** `741906283517` is ungrouped; redemption is on Ledger with no app copy button. **Mechanism/impact:** unchunked recall/transcription is burdensome. **Recovery:** normal selection/copy/paste, notes, screenshot, and backtracking work. **Evidence:** `circular.png`.
11. **Recall rather than recognition** — *Memory 2, pp. 7–18.* **Location:** Grower Notes and catalog wrap selector. **Behavior:** checkout-relevant choices show only codes; full mappings live on a separate page. **Mechanism/impact:** user must retain or externally record `WG17`. **Recovery:** Notes remain accessible through menu and browser back. **Evidence:** `notes.png`, `catalog.png`.
12. **Working-memory comparison burden** — *Memory 1, pp. 19–25.* **Location:** Flight Ledger. **Behavior:** 18 cards reveal price/window in individual dockets without a comparison table. **Mechanism/impact:** comparing options requires revisits and retained attributes. **Recovery:** opened dockets can be revisited and selection persists. **Evidence:** `delivery.png`.
13. **Unnecessarily difficult language** — *Reading and Language, pp. 13–19.* **Location:** catalog, Flight, Ledger prose. **Behavior:** truthful instructions use verbose terms such as “presentation integuments” and “situational particulars.” **Mechanism/impact:** uncommon wording increases interpretation work. **Recovery:** familiar labels, values, and task card give sufficient context. **Evidence:** `checkout.png`.
14. **Poor signposting/reading layout** — *Reading and Language, pp. 8–19.* **Location:** shop informational views. **Behavior:** centered long prose, repetitive sentences, sparse headings, and weak hierarchy impede scanning. **Mechanism/impact:** readers must inspect blocks rather than scan clear landmarks. **Recovery:** task brief and receipt deliberately stay readable. **Evidence:** `facts.png`.
15. **Unnecessary calculation** — *Thinking and Problem Solving, pp. 9–15, 53–57.* **Location:** product, circular, delivery dockets. **Behavior:** unit price, quantity, discount, and charge are distributed; no running total appears until final review. **Mechanism/impact:** evaluating budget requires arithmetic across views. **Recovery:** final review computes exact integer-cent total before confirmation and permits correction. **Evidence:** `checkout.png`.
16. **Poor alternative support** — *Thinking and Problem Solving, pp. 44–57.* **Location:** Flight Ledger. **Behavior:** 18 plausible services are unsorted with no filter/comparison. Only Morning Flight meets both constraints; Queen’s Reserve has the same window but exceeds budget. **Mechanism/impact:** repeated inspection increases search and may produce choice overload (not guaranteed). **Recovery:** every docket is truthful/stable and selectable. **Evidence:** `delivery.png`.
17. **Fitts’ Law** — *Interaction, pp. 4–20.* **Location:** catalog, docket, Ledger progression. **Behavior:** several important desktop controls are stationary 26px-high targets, sometimes far from affected information. **Mechanism/impact:** smaller/farther targets demand more precise movement. **Recovery:** full visible area is clickable; responsive controls remain usable. **Evidence:** `catalog.png`.
18. **Accot–Zhai Steering Law** — *Interaction, pp. 29–38.* **Location:** Old Hive Files → Grower Cabinet → Grower Notes. **Behavior:** a shallow narrow two-level hover corridor requires controlled travel. **Mechanism/impact:** path width and length make steering harder. **Recovery:** continuous corridor plus focus-within and click/tap operation; no timer/disappearance while inside. **Evidence:** `hover-menu.png`.
19. **Disorganized visual search** — *Interaction, pp. 22–28, 34–36.* **Location:** 14-item farm navigation. **Behavior:** useful references mix with meaningful miscellany without alphabetical/semantic ordering. **Mechanism/impact:** serial label inspection is encouraged; no universal memory-limit claim is made. **Recovery:** all destinations are stable and references remain available. **Evidence:** `anti-home.png`.
20. **Weak progress visibility** — *Thinking and Problem Solving, pp. 9–12.* **Location:** flow labels. **Behavior:** Allocation, Presentation, Flight, and Ledger are opaque, with no numbered indicator. **Mechanism/impact:** remaining work is hard to estimate. **Recovery:** coherent links, reopenable brief, final review, and explicit receipt prevent loss or ambiguity at completion. **Evidence:** `checkout.png`, `anti-receipt.png`.
